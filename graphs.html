    hermes.graph

    Graphs

    A KnowledgeGraph is a store to query, a schema to read the ontology from, and preferred prefixes. Stores answer SPARQL 1.1 JSON, so files and endpoints are interchangeable.

    from hermes import KnowledgeGraph
kg = KnowledgeGraph.from_files("ontology.ttl", "data.ttl", schema=["ontology.ttl"])
kg = KnowledgeGraph.from_endpoint("https://dbpedia.org/sparql")
from hermes import benchmarks; kg = benchmarks.graph("corporate")   # data/graphs.yaml
    • Oxigraph (default for files), rdflib, endpoint and browser (the page's Oxigraph under Pyodide) stores; a caching decorator memoizes results and counts executions. Source: stores.py.
    • Statistics are read once for local graphs and one predicate at a time from endpoints (statistics.py).
    • Entity linking matches labels exactly, or by their words (linking.py).
