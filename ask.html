    Demo

    Try: ask a question

    Pick a graph and a question; Hermes runs in your browser and shows every stage. The sample questions replay recorded replies of Llama 3.1 8B; for your own questions, use a model you run or paste its replies.

    What runs where

    Hermes's own Python package runs in your browser under Pyodide, the same wheel the benchmarks run, and its queries run on Oxigraph compiled to WebAssembly, the engine Hermes uses for local graphs. No server of ours is involved. Only the model is not here: Ollama on your computer is detected under My model, a small model can run in the page on WebGPU, or you can paste replies. Your files stay in the page.

    Reading it

    • Ontology slice: what the model is shown of the ontology. Classes and properties are chosen by the question's words, then completed with what the chosen classes use; values and entities are those the question names.
    • Repair: each change, with its reason. Problems that remain are sent back to the model while the budget allows.
    • Plan: the query as relational algebra, as written and as rewritten, with the estimated rows of every operator.
    • Price and spend: what the run was expected to cost before each step, and what it cost. A step that would go over the budget is not taken.
