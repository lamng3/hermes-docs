    hermes.experiments

    Evaluation

    Six benchmarks in one format, a scorer that reproduces every published Text2SPARQL 2026 score, and runs that record their config, commit, answers and cost.

    On this page
    1. Benchmarks
    2. Runs
    3. Results

    Benchmarks

    Text2SPARQL 2026 corporate (CK26) and DBpedia (DB26), their 2025 editions, QALD-9-plus and QALD-10, fetched with hermes data fetch. CK26 is CK25 paraphrased, so CK25 is not a development set for CK26.

    Runs

    hermes run configs/ck26/hermes.yaml
hermes sweep configs/sweeps/hermes.yaml   # Hermes, its ablations, and baselines
hermes report

    Each run writes its config, commit, every prediction with its cost, and metrics under results/. Scores: the official set F1 and F1-nDCG, a strict variant, and the subset whose gold answers reproduce on the local engine.

    Results

    The pipeline's runs are in results/.
