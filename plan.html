    Reading it

    Each line of a plan is one operator: scan a triple pattern, ⋈ a join, σ a filter, π a projection, ⟕ OPTIONAL, γ grouping. est is the planner's estimate of the rows, got what the run produced. The plan executor treats the graph as a black box that answers single-pattern queries, so the join order decides how much is fetched; see Planner.
