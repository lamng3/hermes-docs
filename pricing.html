    hermes.pricing

    Pricing and budgets

    Every model call and every plan is priced before it runs; a budget caps calls, tokens, dollars, graph queries and rows, and a run reports what each stage was expected to cost and what it cost.

    On this page
    1. Prices
    2. Budgets
    3. Pricing graph queries

    Prices

    A Price counts model calls, input and output tokens, dollars when the model has a list price, graph queries and rows fetched. A model call is priced from its prompt (four characters a token) with 400 output tokens; a plan from the planner's estimates of the queries and rows its executor will fetch. Source: budget.py, list prices in prices.py.

    Budgets

    from hermes import Budget, Hermes
answer = Hermes(kg, budget=Budget(llm_calls=3, tokens=20_000, rows=50_000)).ask(question)
answer.spend.to_dict()     # per stage: estimated and actual
answer.error               # "over budget at execute: rows would reach 61,204, over the cap of 50,000"

    A step that would break the budget is not taken: a repair the budget cannot afford is skipped and the problems are reported; a plan over the row cap is not run. BudgetedLLM decorates any model client with the same check.

    Pricing graph queries

    The graph side of a price comes from the planner (see estimates): a plan's rows fetched are estimated scan by scan, with bind joins priced as index accesses, before anything is sent. On the corporate graph the median q-error of the result's row estimate is 1.10; on DBpedia, where statistics come from the endpoint one predicate at a time, 1.41.
